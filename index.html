Common questions

Do you offer in-home tech support in Wellington and Kāpiti?

Yes. We come to your home or business across Wellington, the Kāpiti Coast, Porirua and the Hutt Valley to fix slow computers, set up devices, sort Wi-Fi and email, and remove viruses.

How much does tech support cost?

Tech support is $80-140 per hour on a sliding scale, so you can pay what suits your budget. Remote help is available NZ-wide.

Can you build a website for my small business?

Yes. We design custom coded websites and work with WordPress, Squarespace and Wix. Every site is mobile-friendly, fast and set up for Google from day one. Websites are quoted per project after a free chat.

Do you help with Microsoft 365, cybersecurity and AI?

We set up and secure Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, add multi-factor authentication and backups, and introduce practical AI and automation tools for small businesses.

How quickly can you help?

Get in touch by phone, text or the form and we'll reply within one business day, often sooner for urgent tech problems.
